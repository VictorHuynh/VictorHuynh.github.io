## Analog Audio Amplifier Speaker System

**Project description:** In this project, I designed and built a Class-D audio amplifier to drive a speaker, including simulation, prototyping, and characterization. I tested the system's performance across frequency, harmonic distortion, and efficiency, and integrated with Bluetooth and other audio sources for real-world playback.
