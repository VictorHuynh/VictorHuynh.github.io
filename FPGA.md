## Wave-Table Music Synthesizer on FPGA

**Project description:** In this project, I implemented a wave-table music synthesizer on the PYNQ FPGA board using Verilog and synthesized and implemented it with Xilinx Vivado. My system reads songs from memory, looks up note frequencies in a ROM, and generates pure sine wave tones for playback through a speaker. I also display the audio waveform in real time on a VGA/HDMI monitor, with the waveform centered horizontally and adjustable in scale using FPGA inputs.
